# Complete step preparation audit

Reviewed on 2026-10-26: **154 current skills and all 384 active steps**. The 36 archived earlier teaching pages are retained unchanged.

The former Get ready renderer joined the skill objective and shared setup. The new Setup uses only the step-specific preparation below. Original shared text is retained in the review JSON; cue, teaching, criteria and prerequisite fingerprints are checked by the generator and unit suite. This review covers preparation suitability, specificity, objective separation, placement, consistency with the teaching, and avoidance of unnecessary duplication.

Preparation is original editorial work using the existing lessons and their references. Safety cross-checks used [Dogs Trust target training](https://www.dogstrust.org.uk/dog-advice/training/tips-tricks/target-training), [basket muzzles](https://www.dogstrust.org.uk/dog-advice/training/outdoors/muzzle-training), [harnesses](https://www.dogstrust.org.uk/dog-advice/training/outdoors/harness-training), [RSPCA training guidance](https://www.rspca.org.uk/adviceandwelfare/pets/dogs/training), and [VCA dental-care training](https://vcahospitals.com/know-your-pet/successful-toothbrushing-training-for-happy-dental-care). These support preparation principles and safety; they do not validate every original exercise.
